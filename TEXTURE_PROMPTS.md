# Texture Prompts

These prompts are set up for the new candy titanium item set. Save each generated texture as a `16x16` or `32x32` PNG with transparency and replace the matching file in `src/main/resources/assets/sweetapocalpysebiome/textures/item/`.

General guidance for every prompt:
- Style: Minecraft item sprite, clean pixel art, readable silhouette, centered subject, transparent background
- Lighting: soft top-left highlight, crisp contrast, no background scene
- Output: one item only, no text, no frame, no drop shadow outside the sprite
- Palette: silver titanium metal mixed with hot-pink candy stripes to match the current ingot look

## `titanium_candy_ingot.png`
Create a Minecraft-style pixel art item sprite of a titanium candy ingot. The ingot should be silver metallic with bright pink candy swirls and glossy sugar-like highlights. Keep it chunky, readable, and slightly magical. Transparent background. No text.

## `enhanced_candy_ingot.png`
Create a Minecraft-style pixel art item sprite of an enhanced titanium candy ingot. It should look like an upgraded version of a silver ingot with vivid pink candy veins, brighter white shine, a faint cyan enchanted glow, and a more premium refined shape. Transparent background. No text.

## `titanium_candy_sword.png`
Create a Minecraft-style pixel art sword sprite made from titanium candy. Silver metallic blade with hot-pink candy striping running through the blade, dark handle, and a candy crystal accent in the guard. Keep the silhouette clean and readable as a handheld Minecraft weapon. Transparent background. No text.

## `titanium_candy_pickaxe.png`
Create a Minecraft-style pixel art pickaxe sprite made from titanium candy. Silver metal head with bright pink candy veins, sturdy dark handle, and a slightly glossy sugared finish. Make it look strong, rare, and clearly recognizable as a pickaxe in a small pixel-art icon. Transparent background. No text.

## `titanium_candy_axe.png`
Create a Minecraft-style pixel art axe sprite made from titanium candy. Broad silver axe head with pink candy striping, dark grip, and shiny sugary highlights. Keep it bold and easy to read at small size. Transparent background. No text.

## `titanium_candy_shovel.png`
Create a Minecraft-style pixel art shovel sprite made from titanium candy. Silver shovel scoop with pink candy bands, dark handle, polished shine, and a slightly whimsical candy-metal feel. Keep the silhouette simple and readable in Minecraft inventory scale. Transparent background. No text.

## Optional `candy_compressor.png`
Create a Minecraft-style pixel art machine item called a candy compressor. Small handheld item icon showing a compact metal-and-candy device with silver body, pink candy core, and simple mechanical details. It should feel like a crafted utility item, not food. Transparent background. No text.
