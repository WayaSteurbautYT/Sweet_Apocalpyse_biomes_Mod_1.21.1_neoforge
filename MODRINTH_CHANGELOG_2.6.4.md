# Sweet Apocalypse Biomes 2.6.4

Minecraft 1.21.1 | NeoForge 21.1.232

## New Advancement Tabs

- **Food: First Bite, Last Chance** — Find a Chocolate Bar, discover the Cookie Notch Apple, then try a Corrupted Cookie.
- **Purification: Clean Start, Dirty World** — Find the Purifier, obtain Purify Ore, and carry a Purification Potion.
- **Weapons: Crunch Time** — Craft a Cookie Sword, upgrade to a Titanium Candy Sword, improve it, and forge a Chocolate Fragment Sword.
- **Enchantments: A Little Enchanted** — Enchant an item, then apply Candy Crusher to a tool.
- **Exploration: Portal Problems** — Craft the Portal Igniter, enter the Candy Dimension, and bring back Candy Titanium Ore.
- **Mining: Dig Into the Sweet Stuff** — Mine Candy Titanium Ore, make Titanium Candy Ingots, and craft Enhanced Candy Titanium.
- **Building and Crafting: Build Before It Bites** — Get Chocolate Planks and craft a Titanium Candy Pickaxe.
